Daniel Levine

Yale–Boehringer Ingelheim Biomedical Data Science Postdoctoral Fellow

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the van Dijk Lab at Yale University, mentored by David van Dijk. My research interests include continual learning, 0-shot generalization, biologically grounded learning, biomedical foundation models, and AI agents.
